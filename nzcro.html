Independent reviews of Department of Conservation decisions

The New Zealand Conservation Review Organisation (NZCRO) is a not‑for‑profit, independent body that reviews decisions made by the Department of Conservation when individuals, iwi, hapū, community groups, or organisations seek a review. Our process is free for complainants and focused on fairness, tikanga Māori, natural justice, and transparency.

Our purpose

Around one third of Aotearoa New Zealand is public conservation land and waters. Decisions over these taonga require transparency and robust checks and balances. NZCRO fills a gap in the current system by providing an expert, timely, and independent review of Department of Conservation (DOC) decisions.

Independent
Governed by a multi‑disciplinary board; funded independently; free from DOC direction.
Expert
Panels include specialists in administrative law, planning/resource management, ecology, tikanga Māori, and te ao Māori.
Transparent
We publish reasoned determinations (with appropriate privacy protections) and annual reporting against KPIs.

How it works

Step 1
Lodge
Submit a review request. We triage for jurisdiction and urgency.
Step 2
Engage
We notify DOC and relevant parties (incl. mana whenua) and invite responses/kōrero.
Step 3
Review
Panel assesses process, facts, policy, and tikanga. Mediation may be offered.
Step 4
Publish
Written determination with reasons and any recommendations.
Scope & jurisdiction

NZCRO reviews DOC decisions (or failures to decide) affecting public conservation land and waters, concessions, permits, access arrangements, authorisations, and statutory advocacy. We do not replace courts or tribunals. Our determinations are non‑binding but carry persuasive weight and are published for accountability.

Code of Ethics & Reporting

Independence & Integrity
  • No direction from DOC or Ministers; funding arrangements published annually
  • Conflict‑of‑interest register; panelists recuse where appropriate
Tikanga Māori & Te Tiriti
  • Embed tikanga Māori in process and decision‑making
  • Engage early with mana whenua; uphold partnership principles
Fairness & Natural Justice
  • Hear both sides; provide reasons; enable correction of factual error
  • Accessibility: plain language, alternate formats, free lodging
Transparency & Data
  • Publish determinations (with necessary redactions)
  • Annual report with KPIs: timeliness, uptake, implementation of recommendations

Board & Panels

NZCRO is governed by a board with expertise in law, planning, ecology, tikanga Māori, governance, and public administration. Review panels are convened per matter from a standing roster.

Chair (Law)
Senior counsel in administrative and public law; specialist in ombudsman‑style review.
Member (Planning)
RMA/SPA planner with conservation management experience and tribunal appearances.
Member (Tikanga Māori)
Kaiārahi with deep tikanga expertise and iwi engagement leadership.
Member (Ecology)
Field ecologist with protected species and habitats expertise.
Member (Governance)
Independent director; audit and risk chair.
Registrar
Leads intake, triage, timetabling, and publication.

Frequently asked questions

Is NZCRO part of the Government or DOC?
No. NZCRO is an independent, not‑for‑profit body. We are not part of DOC, and DOC does not direct our work.
Are your determinations binding?
Our determinations are non‑binding, like private ombudsman schemes. They provide persuasive guidance and public accountability.
Who can request a review?
Individuals, iwi/hapū, community groups, NGOs, and organisations affected by a DOC decision or delay may apply.
What standards do you apply?
Administrative law principles (fairness, reasonableness, lawfulness), relevant statutes and policies, tikanga Māori, and best‑practice conservation science.
Do you charge fees?
Lodging a review is free for complainants. We may recover costs for optional mediation or expert testing by agreement.
Can you help urgently?
Yes. We triage time‑critical matters where irreversible effects may occur and can issue expedited recommendations.
